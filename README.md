### 🧭 A little about me

I write code, and I write about the stuff underneath the code — why we get stuck, how we change, and what actually makes progress stick.

- 🔭 **Building [Systivia](https://systivia.com)**, a habit tracker that tracks *who you're becoming*, not just streaks
- 🫂 **Running [Systivia Circle](https://systivia.com/dis)**, a small Discord community for people building habits through identity
- ✍️ **Writing** about awareness, habits and self-improvement on [my blog](https://raozaeem.com/blog) and in my [newsletter](https://raozaeem.com/newsletter)
- 🎓 **Studying** BS Computer Science at Sindh Madressatul Islam University, Karachi 🇵🇰
- 🐧 **Daily driver:** CachyOS · KDE Plasma · Neovim (LazyVim) · Fish + Starship
- 🐱 **Fun fact:** I like having cats as my profile picture

> *"Awareness isn't the last step of healing, but it is the first."*
